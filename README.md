# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard using Power BI

## 📖 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the **Superstore sales dataset** and developing an interactive business intelligence dashboard using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance by analyzing **sales, profit, customers, products, categories, sub-categories, regions, states, and order dates**.

The project includes **data preparation, data cleaning, data modeling, and interactive data visualization**. The final dashboard helps users understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, and sales trends over time.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

📊 Analyze overall Superstore sales performance.
💰 Evaluate sales and profit generated by the business.
👥 Analyze customer-wise sales performance.
📦 Identify top-performing products and sub-categories.
🏷️ Compare sales across different product categories.
🌍 Analyze sales and profit across regions and states.
📈 Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
🎚️ Develop interactive filters and slicers.
📋 Present business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

# 📊 Dashboard Features

## 💰 KPI Analysis

The dashboard provides important Key Performance Indicators including:

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Orders**
* **Total Customers**
* **Total Quantity**
* **Profit Margin %**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

## 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

A **line chart** is used to display sales performance over time.

This visualization helps identify:

* Monthly sales growth
* Declining sales periods
* Seasonal patterns
* High-performing months
* Low-performing months

## 🏷️ Sales by Category

A chart is used to compare sales across major product categories such as:

* Furniture
* Office Supplies
* Technology

This helps identify which category contributes the most to overall sales.

## 🔖 Sales by Sub-Category

A bar chart displays sales performance across different sub-categories.

This helps identify the most successful product segments.

## 🌍 Sales by Region

A visualization compares sales performance across different regions:

* Central
* East
* South
* West

This helps identify regions generating higher sales and areas requiring improvement.

## 💵 Profit by Region

A chart is used to compare **profit generated by each region**.

This helps determine whether high-sales regions are also generating strong profits.

## 📍 Sales by State

A geographical or bar-chart visualization can be used to analyze sales across different states.

This helps identify high-performing and low-performing states and regional opportunities.

## 📦 Product-wise Sales Analysis

A bar chart displays sales generated by individual products.

This helps identify:

🏆 Top-performing products
📉 Low-performing products
💰 Products contributing significantly to revenue

## 👥 Top 10 Customers by Sales

A column or bar chart displays the **Top 10 Customers based on total sales**.

This helps identify the customers who contribute significantly to business revenue.

## 🔢 Quantity Analysis

The dashboard analyzes the quantity of products sold across:

* Categories
* Sub-categories
* Regions
* Products

This provides additional information about product demand.

## 💰 Sales and Profit Analysis

Sales and profit can be compared to identify products or categories that generate high or low profitability.
